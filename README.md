## The thesis

SEVENFOLD followed one molecule. **EIGHTFOLD follows the machine that makes the
next one.**

In Jay's framing, which is the scope of this case:

> All drugs. All drugs in production, and the ones the market will come up with to
> replace them and prey on addicts. And the lack of help for them. **A country that
> leaves 42 million behind.**

Three claims, each already carrying evidence out of SEVENFOLD:

**1. The market always produces a replacement, and enforcement is what selects for
it.** SEVENFOLD Finding 55 traces this at national scale: prescription opioids
controlled in 2010, heroin deaths rise **3,036 → 15,469**, heroin interdicted,
fentanyl, fentanyl scheduled, nitazenes and carfentanil. Every step a supply-side
victory. Every step a more lethal substitute. **107,941 overdose deaths in a single
year.** Finding 20 catches the same loop mid-turn on a smaller scaffold: the
manufacturing claim on the unscheduled successor to 7-OH was filed **2023-09-20**
and the federal record still holds **zero** assessments of it.

**2. It works because the demand is never touched.** SAMHSA's own 2024 survey,
verified from the primary in `exhibits/nsduh/`:

| | |
|---|---|
| Had a substance use disorder, 2024 | **48.4 million** (16.8% of people 12+) |
| Needed treatment | 52.6 million |
| **Received it** | **5.9 million (12.3%)** |
| **Did not** | **42.4 million (87.7%)** |
| Of people with **SEVERE** SUD, received nothing | **72.1%** |
| Prevalence change, 2021 to 2024 | **none** |

**Seventy-two percent of Americans with a severe substance use disorder got
nothing.** That figure survives the fair objection that most of the 48.4 million is
classified mild — state it that way and it cannot be waved off.

And prevalence **has not moved since 2021**, through the entire fentanyl response.
Finding 51 found the same thing across four birth cohorts back to 1900: the
relationship between childhood adversity and addiction does not respond to anything
done at the supply end.

**3. Those 42 million are not a side effect of the market. They are the market.**
A population that is dependent, untreated and has nowhere else to go is a
guaranteed customer for whatever is legal this year. **[I] That is the mechanism
this case exists to document.**

## Evidence standard

Inherited from SEVENFOLD without modification. Full version in `METHOD.md`. Every
rule exists because SEVENFOLD broke it once and got caught.

- Tag **[P]** primary / **[S]** secondary / **[I]** inference / **[U]** unverified,
  as you write.
- **No negative without a control.** Run a known-present term first and record it.
- **Verify the instrument.** Two views of the same aggregation can agree and both be
  wrong — L-05 §4 is the worked example, where a cross-check passed and the number
  was still false.
- **Retain failures.** `exhibits/_blocked/`. Never counted as evidence of absence.
- **Refuted claims get a file**, before they propagate.
- **Correct in place**, original preserved as a dated `.bak-`. Correct your own
  corrections too.
- **Write for a hostile reader.** State your weakness before they find it.
