# Verified from this document, not from recollection or secondary reporting:
#   48.4 million people aged 12+ (16.8%) had an SUD in the past year
#   27.9M alcohol use disorder, 28.2M drug use disorder, 7.7M both
#   52.6 million (18.2%) NEEDED substance use treatment
#   5.9 million (12.3%) RECEIVED it
#   42.4 million (87.7%) DID NOT
#   severe SUD: only 27.9% (2.9M) received treatment -> ~72% of severe got nothing
#   moderate: 11.8% received.  mild: 6.4% received.
#   SUD prevalence showed NO CHANGE 2021 to 2024.
#
# CAVEAT ON TRENDS, from the report itself: treatment questions were revised for
# the 2022 and again for the 2024 NSDUH, so "4 years of data are not available to
# assess trends in the receipt of substance use treatment." Do not trend this.
