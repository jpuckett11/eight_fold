This page tallies the reported values of the major opioid settlements to generate an official Global Settlement Tracker sum.*

The pie chart and table below track key details from the major opioid settlements reached between U.S. state and local governments and pharmaceutical opioid manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and consultants.

  • Click each pie slice to view detailed information.

  • Use the legend to filter the pie by specific companies.

*as of April 14, 2026:

Pending finalization of Albertsons’ $774 million opioid settlement, the Global Settlement Total will be $58.58 billion.
