## 6. What this finding does NOT claim

- **It does not say scheduling is wrong or should stop.** These are potent opioids
  reaching people who did not order them. **CFSRE says "appears to have accelerated,"
  not "caused," and this file uses their word.**
- **The case counts are presumptive positives** from one laboratory network's
  datamining, not confirmed causes of death.
- **The geography is NMS Labs' client base**, stated by CFSRE and repeated here.
- **The causal claim is theirs, not this file's.** No independent correlation has been
  computed — see Finding 15 §5 for the design that would be needed and the detection
  control it requires.
- **"About four years after brorphine controls"** is CFSRE's characterisation; the
  interval is not independently verified here.
