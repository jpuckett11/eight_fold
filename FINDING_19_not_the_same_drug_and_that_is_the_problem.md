# EIGHTFOLD / FINDING 19
## Acend is not rebranded 7-OH. The certificates prove it, and that partly refutes the simple reading. What the certificate of the successor product *does* show is 9-hydroxycorynantheidine sitting on the analyte panel, marked N/T — Not Tested.

**Status:** **CONFIRMED [P]** from eight certificates of analysis published by the
manufacturer, with a unit defect found and reconciled before any figure was used.
**This finding partly contradicts the simplest version of the case's own suspicion and
says so in §3.**
**Analyst:** Aegis, for Obsidian Watch Group. **Date:** 2026-08-31.
**Opened at Jay Puckett's instruction: "pull the acend coa and compare to the 7-hydro
one."**

Exhibits in `exhibits/coa/`. Issuing laboratory on all eight: **Wonderland Labs,
Vista CA.** Client: **Real Botanicals, Fort Mill SC.**

---

## 1. The unit defect, resolved before anything was claimed [P]

Every COA heads its potency column **"Total %"** and then labels the unit **"mg/g"** or
**"mg/tab."** Those cannot both be right.

**Read as percent of product weight, every value lands on its own product label:**

| COA figure | × product weight | = | Product label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.85% | 502 mg | **9.3 mg** | 10 mg |
| 7.28% | 517 mg | **37.6 mg** | 40 mg |
| 15.30% | 625 mg | **95.6 mg** | 100 mg |
| 2.15% | 580 mg | **12.5 mg** | Acend summary says "Mitragynine Content: 12 mg/tab" |

**The column is percent and the unit labels are wrong.** Read literally as mg/g the
numbers are absurd. **This finding uses the reconciled mg-per-tablet figures.** Had it
not been checked, the Acend COA's apparent contradiction — 2.15 in the table against
12 in the summary — would have been reported as a discrepancy. **It is not one. It is
a labelling error in the certificate.**

## 3. What this refutes, stated plainly

**The 7-Hydro products are 7-OH isolates.** Mitragynine is essentially absent —
0.05 mg in the 10mg tablet, **not detected at all** in the 100mg — and every other
kratom alkaloid reads N/D. **These are not kratom extracts. They are purified
7-hydroxymitragynine in a tablet.**

**Acend is the inverse and it is a genuine kratom alkaloid extract.** 7-OH **below the
limit of detection** (stated interim LOD, 1.0 ppm). Mitragynine present. And crucially
the *other* naturally occurring alkaloids — speciogynine, paynantheine, speciociliatine
— are present, which is what a plant-derived extract looks like and what a synthetic
isolate does not.

**[I] So Acend is not the same drug relabelled, and this file will not say it is.**
The suspicion that the successor product is the banned product under a new name is
**not supported by these certificates.** That is the honest result and it is reported
because it is the result.

**Two things survive that, and they are in §4 and §5.**

## 4. The line that matters: 9-OH is on the panel and was not run [P]

The Acend COA uses method **MIT-EVO_R2026-PT**, an **eleven-analyte** panel. The 2025
7-Hydro COAs used **MIT-EVO_R2025-01**, a five or six-analyte panel. **The panel
expanded.** What was added:

| # | Analyte | Acend result |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **9-Hydroxycorynantheidine** | **N/T — Not Tested** |
| 9 | 10-Hydroxycorynantheidine | **N/T** |
| 10 | 7-Acetoxymitragynine | **N/T** |
| 11 | **9-Fluoro-7-hydroxymitragynine** | **N/T** |

**SEVENFOLD Finding 27 established, from human liver S9 preparations (PMID 39299374),
that 9-hydroxycorynantheidine is formed from mitragynine at 36% of total metabolism and
"displays similar pharmacologic activity."** It is named in no federal scheduling
instrument. **Finding 20 §3 recorded that FDA's adverse-event system returns zero
reports for it against a working control of 879 for kratom.**

**Here it is on the certificate of analysis of the successor product, printed, numbered,
and left unmeasured.**

**[I] The laboratory built the capability. The panel lists the compound. Nobody ran
it.** This finding makes **no claim of intent** — N/T may mean no validated reference
standard, no client request, or cost. **What is established is that the analyte exists
on the method, and the result is blank.**

**And note analyte 11.** *9-Fluoro-7-hydroxymitragynine* is a **fluorinated** 7-OH
analog — a designer modification, not a plant constituent. **Its presence on a
commercial kratom panel means the laboratory expects to encounter it.** That is
Finding 16's substitution pattern appearing on a testing menu before it appears in a
scheduling order.

## 5. What the customer actually swallows [I]

**A 200mg-labelled Acend tablet delivers roughly 47 mg of total alkaloids, of which
about 12.5 mg is mitragynine.**

**Mitragynine is the precursor.** Finding 27: the body converts it to
9-hydroxycorynantheidine at 36% of total metabolism via CYP3A4. Finding 56: **7-OH is
itself a human metabolite of mitragynine.**

**So the scheduling action removed the metabolite from the shelf and left the
precursor.** The 7-OH tablet delivered 9.3 to 95.6 mg of a scheduled compound directly.
The Acend tablet delivers 12.5 mg of the compound the body uses to make it — plus
9-OH, which no instrument counts and which this certificate did not measure.

**[I] That is a materially different product with a materially different dose, and this
finding does not pretend the exposure is equivalent.** It is not. **The point is
narrower: the regime measures what is in the tablet, and the pharmacology happens
after it is swallowed.** Finding 27 said so in March. The certificate is the commercial
proof.

## 6. What this finding does NOT claim

- **It does not allege mislabelling, adulteration or any violation.** The COAs are
  published voluntarily and the 7-OH result is below LOD.
- **It does not claim Acend is unsafe or equivalent to the 7-OH products.** §5 says the
  opposite about dose.
- **It does not claim the N/T entries were deliberate.** §4 lists the innocent
  explanations first.
- **These are the manufacturer's own certificates**, from a laboratory OWG has not
  audited. **No independent assay has been performed.** The whole comparison rests on
  documents the seller chose to publish.
- **One-lot snapshots.** The Acend COA is a single lot dated 07/26/2026.
- **The unusual alkaloid ratio is noted, not interpreted.** Speciogynine at 22.1 mg
  exceeds mitragynine at 12.5 mg, which is not the ratio of ordinary leaf. **This file
  does not know what that means and does not speculate.**
