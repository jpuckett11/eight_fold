# L-08 — What is in production now

**Status: OPEN, unworked. Opened 2026-08-31.** Not citable.

## The question

The current landscape of substances being manufactured and sold to a dependent
population, legally or in the grey market, **before** any of them becomes the next
scheduling action.

SEVENFOLD Finding 30 is the template at one plant: a table of what a scheduling
order does **not** reach, built by reading the rule text against the compound list.
It found a **mu-opioid full agonist** (corynoxine) and a **major human metabolite**
(9-OH) both lawful on the day the order took effect. Finding 29 documents products
already built around one of them, sold as "Cat's Claw", with severe overdoses
reported by a state poison control center and a death.

**L-08 generalises that table.**

## What would answer it

1. **Per drug class: what is scheduled, and what sits adjacent and uncontrolled.**
2. **Retail reality.** What is actually on shelves and on which channels. SEVENFOLD's
   `exhibits/marketing/` captures are the method.
3. **The naming problem.** Products rename constantly. Finding 29's substitution was
   only visible because a poison control center and a coroner connected a brand name
   to a compound.

## Controls

- Absence of a product from a search is **not** absence from the market. SEVENFOLD
  states this explicitly in Finding 20 s7 and it must travel with any negative here.
- Do not infer a compound from a product name. Assay or agency finding only.
