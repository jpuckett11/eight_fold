# L-02 — Who holds what

**Status: WORKED for the US House, 2026-08-31. The result is a NULL, and the null
is the finding. Senate NOT covered.**

---

## 1. The question

SEVENFOLD Finding 01 found a serving cabinet secretary holding **$500,001 to
$1,000,000** of a private kratom manufacturer, absent from both attachments to his
ethics agreement while he certified completing every required divestiture.

**L-02 asks whether that is typical.** Does the wider addiction economy — private
detention, behavioral health chains, opioid-treatment pharma, drug testing and
offender monitoring — appear in congressional disclosures the same way?

## 2. Method [P]

Searched the House disclosure corpus assembled for SEVENFOLD: **1,117 filings**,
CY2024 and CY2025 annual and amended reports (types O and A), text-layer extraction
plus OCR for image-only scans.

**Control:** `Northwestern Mutual|Vanguard|Fidelity` fires on **586 of 1,117
filings (52%)**. The corpus is searchable; the negatives below are real.

**Entities were tiered before searching, and the tiering is the method.** Finding 01
mattered because Botanic Tonics is a **private LLC** — not purchasable on an
exchange — held at high value, in the industry the holder was acting on. A member
holding Abbott through an index fund is not a tie to anything. Reporting the two
together would be dishonest.

## 3. Tier 1: niche, private or sector-pure entities

Searched: GEO Group, CoreCivic, Management & Training, Acadia Healthcare, BayMark,
Pinnacle Treatment, American Addiction Centers, Summit BHC, Recovery Centers of
America, Behavioral Health Group, Indivior, Alkermes, Braeburn, Camurus, Emergent
BioSolutions, Averhealth, Sentinel Offender, BI Incorporated, Track Group, Attenti,
SCRAM / Alcohol Monitoring, Securus, ViaPath / GTL, Keefe, Wellpath / Corizon,
Purdue, Mallinckrodt, Endo.

**Four hits, all weak:**

| Member | Holding | Value | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cohen, Steve (TN09) | Emergent BioSolutions (EBS) | $100,001+ | Stephens SEP IRA |
| Onder, Robert (MO03) | GEO Group (GEO) | $1,001-$15,000 | Schwab SEP |
| Teixeira, Mark (TX21) | CoreCivic notes | $2,501-$5,000 | revocable trust |
| McCartney, Rick (AZ01) | Acadia Healthcare (ACHC) | $1,001-$15,000 | — |

**[I] None is Finding 01 shaped, and the difference is not one of degree.** All four
are **publicly traded** equities or notes in ordinary brokerage and retirement
accounts. Three are trivial sums. None is a private company, none sits in a vehicle
implying a relationship, none is missing from an ethics filing.

## 4. Tier 2: mega-caps, context only, NOT ties

Abbott 37 members · CVS 24 · McKesson 17 · Cencora 12 · Cardinal Health 10 ·
LabCorp 7 · Quest 6 · Walgreens 5 · Universal Health Services 4 · Teva 3.

**Index-fund constituents and opioid-settlement defendants that appear in any
diversified portfolio. They establish nothing and must never be cited as a
conflict.** Recorded so a future analyst does not "discover" them.

## 5. What the null establishes

**[I] The Mullin position is anomalous, not representative.** Across 1,117 House
filings there is no second instance of the pattern: a materially large, privately
held stake in an addiction-economy company, held by a member acting on that
industry.

**That strengthens Finding 01.** The obvious defense of Mullin is that everyone in
Washington holds something like this. **On the House record, they do not.**

## 6. What is NOT established, and one gap is large

- **The Senate is not covered at all.** This matters more than everything above,
  because **Mullin was a Senator.** Senate disclosures run through the Senate EFD
  system, which gates behind a session agreement and needs its own harness. **The
  one chamber where the known instance occurred is the chamber left unsearched.**
- **Annual reports only.** Periodic transaction reports list trades, not holdings.
- **Name matching only.** A holding inside an LLC, trust or partnership with a
  neutral name is invisible. Finding 01's subject was findable only because the LLC
  was named on the form — a differently named vehicle would have hidden it.
- **No beneficial-ownership tracing.** ~200 line items per filing were not run to
  underlying holders.
- **Two filing years only.**
