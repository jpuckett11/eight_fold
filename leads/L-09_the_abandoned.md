# L-09 — The abandoned

**Status: OPEN, unworked. Opened 2026-08-31.** Not citable.
**Anchor exhibit already captured: `exhibits/nsduh/`.**

## The question

**42.4 million people.** Established [P] from SAMHSA's 2024 NSDUH:

| | |
|---|---|
| Had an SUD in 2024 | 48.4 million (16.8% of people 12+) |
| Received treatment | 5.9 million (12.3%) |
| **Did not** | **42.4 million (87.7%)** |
| Of those with **severe** SUD, received nothing | **72.1%** |
| Prevalence change 2021-2024 | **none** |

**L-09 asks what is spent on them, where, and by whom** — and pairs the national
figure with per-state numbers so the gap is locatable rather than rhetorical.

## Why this line is the center of the case

**[I] The untreated population is not a consequence of the market. It is the
market.** A dependent, untreated population with nowhere to go is a guaranteed
customer for whatever is legal this year. Every other line in EIGHTFOLD describes
supply; this one describes why supply always finds a buyer.

## What would answer it

1. **State-level SUD prevalence and treatment gap.** NSDUH publishes state estimates
   separately from the national report.
2. **Treatment capacity against need.** SAMHSA's N-SSATS / behavioral health
   treatment locator gives facility counts and capacity by state.
3. **The barriers data.** The 2024 NSDUH carries a section on *reasons for not
   receiving treatment*. That is the closest thing to the answer in the government's
   own words and it has not been read yet.
4. **Pair with L-01 and L-05.** Enforcement per state, prevention per state,
   abandoned per state.

## Controls and caveats that travel with every use

- **Do not trend the treatment numbers.** The report states that treatment questions
  were revised for 2022 and again for 2024, so "4 years of data are not available to
  assess trends in the receipt of substance use treatment." Prevalence may be
  trended; receipt may not.
- **Most of the 48.4 million is classified mild.** Say so first. The severe figure
  (72.1% untreated) is the one that survives the objection.
- 42.4 million is **all substances**, not one drug and not kratom.
